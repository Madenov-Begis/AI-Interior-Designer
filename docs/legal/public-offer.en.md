## 7. Service delivery

7.1. To generate the User:

1. creates or opens a project;
2. uploads a photo of the room;
3. Adds a description, style, visual markup and references;
4. checks the cost of the operation;
5. presses the generation start button.

7.2. The service checks the format, size and content of the image, the available balance and technical restrictions.

7.3. After accepting the task, the Service reserves the required number of credits and places the operation in the queue.

7.4. The service for a specific generation is considered to be rendered after assigning the `SUCCEEDED` status to the task and providing the result in the personal account.

7.5. The generation time is indicative and depends on the file size, load and external AI providers. The interface can display the current status of the task.

7.6. The user can cancel the task only in the status available in the interface. If you cancel successfully, your reserved credits will be automatically refunded.

7.7. In case of a technical generation error, the written-off or reserved credits are automatically returned to the balance. A repeat of the operation is launched by the User separately.
