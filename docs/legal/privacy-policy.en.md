## 2. General definitions

2.1. **Personal data** — information related to a specific individual or allowing him to be identified.

2.2. **Processing** — any action with personal data, including collection, recording, systematization, storage, modification, use, provision, cross-border transfer, depersonalization and destruction.

2.3. **User-generated content** — photos of premises uploaded by the user, visual markup, references, links, text instructions, as well as the generated results.

2.4. **Processor** — a provider of infrastructure or software service that processes data on behalf of the Operator and within the agreed goals.

## 3. What data we process

### 3.1. Account details

When you log in via Google and use the Service, the following may be processed:

- internal user ID;
- first and last name;
- display name;
- email address;
- profile photo;
- identifiers required for authorization;
- date of registration and last login;
- time zone;
- account status and role.

The Operator does not receive or store the password from the Google Account.

### 3.2. User content

To provide services, the following are processed:

- photos of the premises;
- visual marks and masks on images;
- reference images;
- URLs of imported references;
- text descriptions of the desired interior;
- the selected style and format;
- initial and finalized generation results;
- the history of versions and the relationship between generations;
- technical characteristics of images.

3.3. The user must not upload images of people, documents, address plates, personal photos, images of minors or other information of third parties, if he does not have a legal basis and the necessary consents for such processing.

3.4. The operator does not use photos and user requests to train its own AI models, unless the user has separately and explicitly agreed to such use. The procedure for the use of data by an external AI provider is determined by the Operator's contract with this provider and its applicable data processing conditions.

### 3.5. Project and usage data

The following can be processed:

- name and status of the project;
- date of creation and update of the project;
- number of generations;
- task statuses, processing duration and error codes;
- the cost of operations in credits;

- transaction ID of the payment provider;
- information necessary for return and accounting.

Ruvie does not request or store full payment-card details. Those details will be processed by the future payment provider on its secure payment page or form. Before the first payment, the User will receive information about the applicable provider and a link to its data-processing rules; this Policy will be supplemented with the provider's name and the applicable processing rules.

### 3.7. Technical data

When accessing the Service, the following can be automatically processed:

- IP address
- the date and time of the query
- the address of the open page;
- type of browser, device and operating system;
- technical identifiers of the session;
- authorization cookies;
- security logs, network errors and diagnostic information.

### 3.8. Support requests

We process name, email, message content, attachments, and message history when a user contacts support.

## 6. How AI processing works

6.1. To verify that the uploaded image is an interior photo and to generate a result, individual images, visual markup, references and text instructions may be transmitted to Google Cloud Vertex AI.

6.2. Automated processing is used to generate a visual result and technically check the input images. Ruvie does not make decisions based on it that create legal consequences for the user.

6.3. The results are created by probabilistic AI systems. They may contain errors, distortions or visually unreliable elements. The user must independently check the result before using it in repair, construction, purchase of furniture or materials.

6.4. The Service does not replace an architect, engineer, designer or technician and does not perform construction calculations.

## 10. Retention periods

10.1. Account data is stored while the account is active, and after its termination — for the period necessary to comply with the requirements of the law, resolve disputes and protect the rights of the parties.

10.2. Projects, source images, references and results are stored until they are deleted by the user, the account is deleted or the purpose of processing is terminated, unless longer storage is required by law.

10.3. Payment and accounting documents are stored within the time limits established by applicable law.

10.4 Detailed Vercel runtime logs are available for approximately **1 hour**. Generation statuses, duration, technical error codes and financial log are stored in the main database together with the account and projects until their deletion or longer, if necessary to investigate the incident, comply with the requirements of the law or protect the rights of the parties.

10.5. Backups may be retained for up to **30 days** after data is deleted from the primary system and then overwritten or destroyed.

10.6. Deletion of the account is carried out at the request of the user: access is blocked immediately, the data of the main system is deleted or depersonalized according to the approved procedure, and encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.
